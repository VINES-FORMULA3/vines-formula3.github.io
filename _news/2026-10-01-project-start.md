The NSF awards supporting FORMULA3, [No. 2549087](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/show-award/?AWD_ID=2549087) (Tennessee Tech University) and [No. 2549088](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/show-award/?AWD_ID=2549088) (Texas A&M University), started on **October 1, 2026**. They launch a three-year collaboration with New York University, Tampere University, and Aalto University that runs through September 2029.

The first phase of the plan focuses on **Thrust 1**: building hybrid FR3 testbeds with reconfigurable intelligent surfaces and metallic reflector arrays at Texas A&M and Tampere, and running the channel measurement campaign. Those measurements will feed the simulation platform and the AI models developed later in the project. See the full [timeline]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}#timeline).
